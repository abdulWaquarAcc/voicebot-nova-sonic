🔌 Report an Outage

Qn 1. How do I report a power outage?

You can report an outage by calling our contact center and selecting the “Report an Outage” option from the menu.

---

Qn 2. What information do I need to report an outage?

You may need:

* Your name
* Service address
* Account number (if available)
* Description of the issue

---

Qn 3. How can I check the status of a reported outage?

You can check outage status through the automated system or speak with an agent for updates.

---

Qn 4. How long will it take to restore power?

Restoration times vary depending on the cause and severity of the outage. You will receive an estimated restoration time if available.

---

Qn 5. Should I report an outage if my neighbors also have no power?

Yes. Even if neighbors are affected, reporting helps us confirm the scope of the outage.

---

Qn 6. What should I do during a power outage?

* Turn off major appliances
* Keep refrigerator/freezer doors closed
* Avoid downed power lines
* Report the outage immediately

---

Qn 7. How do I report a downed power line?

Stay away and call immediately to report it as an emergency.

---

🏠 Move or Disconnect Service

Qn 8. How do I transfer my electricity service to a new address?

Contact us and select the “Move Service” option. Provide your current and new service address.

---

Qn 9. How far in advance should I request a move?

We recommend contacting us at least 3–5 business days before your move date.

---

Qn 10. How do I disconnect my electricity service?

Call the contact center and select “Disconnect Service.” Provide your account and disconnection date.





---

 Qn 11. Is there a fee for moving or disconnecting service?

Fees may apply depending on your service agreement.

---

Qn 12. Can I schedule a same-day disconnection?

Same-day requests depend on availability and service policies.

---

Qn 13. What happens to my deposit when I disconnect service?

Your deposit (if applicable) may be refunded after your final bill is processed.

---

 🔌 New Connection

Qn 14. How do I request a new electricity connection?

Call the contact center and select “New Connection.” Provide your personal and property details.

---

Qn 15. What documents are required for a new connection?

You may need:

* Valid ID
* Proof of property ownership or lease agreement
* Contact details

---

Qn 16. How long does it take to activate a new connection?

Activation times vary but are typically completed within a few business days.

---

Qn 17. Is a security deposit required for a new connection?

A deposit may be required depending on credit history and service policies.

---

Qn 18. Can I apply for a new connection online?

If online services are available, you may submit your request digitally or through the contact center.

---

 💳 Make a Payment

Qn 19. How can I pay my electricity bill?

You can:

* Use Self Assist automated payment
* Speak to an agent
* Use available online payment options

---

Qn 20. What payment methods are accepted?

Common payment methods include:

* Credit/debit card
* Bank transfer
* Online payment systems




---
Qn 21. Can I set up a payment plan?

Yes. Speak with an agent to discuss available payment arrangement options.

---

Qn 22. What happens if I miss a payment?

Late payments may result in late fees or service disconnection if unpaid.

---

Qn 23. How can I check my current bill amount?

Use Self Assist to hear your balance or select Agent Assist for detailed billing support.

---

Qn 24. Can I get a copy of my bill?

Yes. An agent can provide billing details or resend your statement if available.

---

 📟 Submit Meter Reading

Qn 25. How do I submit my meter reading?

Select the “Submit Meter Reading” option and provide the reading from your meter.

---

Qn 26. When should I submit my meter reading?

Submit your reading on or before your scheduled meter reading date.

---

Qn 27. Where can I find my meter reading?

Your meter reading is displayed on your electricity meter, typically located outside your property.

---

Qn 28. What happens if I don’t submit my meter reading?

Your bill may be estimated based on previous usage.

---

Qn 29. Can I correct a submitted meter reading?

Yes. Contact an agent as soon as possible to correct the information.

---

Qn 30. Why is my bill higher after submitting a meter reading?

A higher bill may reflect:

* Increased electricity usage
* Previous estimated readings being adjusted
* Seasonal consumption changes
